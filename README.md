# 🌱 SGAU Mobile - Urban Agricultural Management System (Urbagro)
---
## 🌿 Overview
SGAU Mobile is the mobile version of the urban agricultural management system, designed to support the organization, control, and optimization of plant cultivation processes in both small-scale and business-oriented environments, directly from your phone.
It focuses on managing foliage, ornamental, and fruit plants, providing tools to track and record key agricultural activities in a centralized, portable, and efficient way.
---
## 🚜 Purpose
This project was created to digitalize and improve the management of agricultural processes in **Urbagro**, a real family business dedicated to urban agriculture, extending the SGAU desktop system with a mobile companion app.
SGAU Mobile replaces manual or outdated methods with a structured system that enhances traceability, reduces errors, and supports better decision-making anytime, anywhere.
---
## ⚙️ Core Features (Available)
- 🌿 Plant management (CRUD by type and category)
- 🌾 Sowing management (process history and methods)
- 👤 User authentication and role-based access
---
## 🛠️ Modules in Development
- 💧 Irrigation control (dates, methods, records)
- 🌱 Fertilization tracking (solid and liquid applications)
---
## 📊 Additional Capabilities (Planned)
- Data filtering and search by plant type or date
- Historical records of agricultural activities
- Input validation to reduce data errors
- Sync with the SGAU desktop system
---
## 🧱 System Design
SGAU Mobile is built with a modular and scalable architecture, allowing it to grow over time as new business needs emerge and new modules are completed.
---
## 🚀 Future Scope
The system is expected to expand beyond operational control, including:
- Advanced production tracking
- Analytics and reporting
- Commercial management features (sales, inventory, and business operations)
- Full parity with the SGAU desktop system
---
## 🧑‍💻 Technologies
- Kotlin
- Android (Android Studio / Gradle)
- SQLite (embedded local database)
---
## 🌍 Goal
To provide a digital solution for urban agriculture that improves efficiency, organization, and scalability for both personal and business use, now accessible on the go through mobile devices.
